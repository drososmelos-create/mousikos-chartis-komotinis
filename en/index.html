Music Memory Map

A map that does not ask us to remember the city as it was. It listens to what insists on returning.

Map: OpenStreetMap · no Google Maps
Historical sites are shown at a documented location where one is available. Historical areas without a single precise point are shown indicatively. The Pomak and Sarakatsani points are cultural reference points north of Komotini, not boundaries of communities.
open a place

Each point is an entrance. Click it. Listen. History is not only found in a place; it is also found in what the body remembers when it hears.

The music links open the sources provided for this project.
